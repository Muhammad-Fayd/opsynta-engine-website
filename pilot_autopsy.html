Every dead pilot dies of one of five things

The autopsy checks all five.

Data quality

The model wasn't wrong — the inputs were. Dirty source data, missing fields, stale context. The pilot starved before it could fail.

Workflow

It worked — and nobody could use it. The seam it automated didn't exist in the workflow that actually runs. Output went to a queue nobody owns.

Adoption

Built, deployed, bypassed. The team routed around it in week two. Usage metrics show the cliff; nobody was assigned to watch for it.

Vendor promise

The demo was real; the production SLA wasn't. Accuracy claims that never survived your data, latencies that broke the process, a roadmap item that was load-bearing.

Unit economics

It worked, and it still lost money. Cost per task never crossed under the human baseline — the pilot was born unprofitable and nobody ran the number.
