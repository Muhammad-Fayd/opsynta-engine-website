Where the 30–60% actually hides

Five levers. The autopsy pulls all five.

Routing

The biggest lever in the research. Capable models doing classification work is the single most common overspend — the audit shows exactly which routes to downgrade and what it saves at your volumes.

Caching

Repeated prompts, billed as new. Prefix-stable requests and unchanged system prompts are cacheable; most mid-market stacks cache nothing. The analysis prices what your repetition is worth.

Dead calls

Nobody remembers creating these routes. Duplicate calls, abandoned features still calling, test traffic in production — every bill carries some, and the inventory makes them visible and deletable.

Prompt weight

The system prompt re-sent 40,000 times. Token weight per call often dwarfs what the task needs. Trimming and restructuring pays per call, multiplied by volume, forever.

Contract

You already qualify for tiers you're not on. The renegotiation pack turns your usage into leverage — the floor every provider has and no mid-market customer knows they've hit.

Questions you should ask

Straight answers.

Isn't this just "switch to a cheaper model"?

If it were, the bill would already be fixed — everyone knows cheaper models exist. The work is the inventory: which of your routes can downgrade without degrading output, which can't, which calls shouldn't exist at all, what your repetition is worth in cache, and what your volumes earn at the negotiating table. "Switch models" is one line in the plan. The other lines are where the money is.

We already use a routing / optimization tool. Why pay you?

Then the autopsy is cheaper for you than for anyone: I audit what the tool is actually doing against your invoices — routes it routes badly, defaults it shipped with, savings it claims versus savings your bills show. Optimization tools are one more layer nobody checks. If the tool is genuinely earning its fee, the verdict says so, and you've bought confirmation for less than a month of your bill.

How does the savings guarantee actually work?

In writing, at booking: the identified savings — the per-lever projections your engineer implements — exceed the $2,500 fee within 90 days of implementation, or your next diagnostic of any type is free.
